# QueueLess – Smart Campus Canteen Ordering

## Project Overview
QueueLess is a proposed mobile application that helps college students order food before their break, track their orders, and collect food without waiting in long queues.

## Problem Statement
Students waste their limited break time standing in canteen queues and cannot easily predict when their food will be ready.

## Proposed Solution
Students can browse the canteen menu, add food to their cart, place orders in advance, choose pickup times, and track order progress.

## Key Features
- View food menu, prices, and availability
- Add items to cart and place orders
- Choose pickup times
- Track order status and queue position
- Receive ready-for-pickup updates
- Canteen staff dashboard
